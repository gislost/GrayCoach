♞ GrayCoach settings

Gray Hub account

Connect your Gray Hub account and the coach uses Gray Hub's Claude, so you don't need your own API key. Each review or question counts toward your Gray Hub daily AI limit. The admin has to turn on GrayCoach for your account.

Connected as .

Claude coach

Get one at console.anthropic.com. The key is stored only in this browser profile (extension storage) and is sent only to api.anthropic.com. Reviews are billed to your API account.

Where reviews open

The side panel stays beside the chess.com or Lichess tab you opened it from, and has a button to move the review to its own tab. Pasted PGNs always open in a tab.

Engine

Stockfish 19 runs in your browser. Deeper is more accurate but slower: a 40-move game takes roughly 30 s / 1–2 min / 3–5 min.

Your accounts

Used to know which side you played, and for the popup's "latest game" buttons. Only public game data is read.

GrayCoach is out. What's new
  1. Download the new version (a .zip file).
  2. Unzip it and copy everything inside into your GrayCoach folder (the one you picked with "Load unpacked"), replacing the old files.
  3. or press the reload arrow ↻ on GrayCoach in your browser's extensions page.
  4. Refresh any chess.com or Lichess tabs you have open.

Your settings and saved reviews stay. Don't click Remove on the extensions page: that deletes them.

Saved games

The last 40 reviewed games and their analyses are kept so they reopen instantly.

Fair play: the coach only reviews finished games. Using engine help during a game you are playing is against the rules on chess.com and Lichess.
